## Methodology

**Random seed:** `42`. **Sampling method:** simple random sample of 100 records drawn uniformly from all 5,475 Excel-matched FIRs, with a stratified top-up (same RNG stream) adding just enough additional records — 26 — to bring every one of 10 required categories (section 3.2/3.4) up to at least 5 members, since several categories (ambiguous-suspected-substance: 2 total in the whole population; non-drug-property leak: 7; KB-alias-gap: 22; multiple-drugs: 153) are too rare for a pure-100 random draw to reliably surface. **Main sample: 126 records** (100 base + 26 top-up). A **separate** 25-record random sample (same seed) was drawn from the 497 crimes left unsupported by the prior quantity-validation fix. **Total actually inspected: 151 records** — full Brief Facts text read for every one (not just the extracted drug field), via `step1_sample.py` / `step2_dump_text.py` in the session scratchpad.

For the 22 of 25 still-unsupported-pool records that fell outside the original 5,479-row Excel reference (the 497 pool was built from a full-database scan, not the Excel sample), Brief Facts and current `drug_extractions` state were read directly from `dopams_cctns_v2` (read-only).

## Edge cases found (real, observed — not hypothetical)

### A. Drug naming patterns
- **OCR/phonetic spelling variants, V1-era**: `gunja`, `gunja` (50kg case, crime `2024049020087`, year 2002: *"illegal possetion of gunja of about 50kg"*), `ganga` (crime `2036049080062`: *"illegally transpotring ganga"*). Below the KB's pg_trgm 0.35 threshold in some cases; confirmed real, not fixed in this pass beyond what pg_trgm already catches (see "KB alias candidates" below).
- **Street/slang terms**: `DAB` (crime `6ad39a8951e985474ef76b37`: *"selling DAB (Highly concentrated cannabis oil)"* — explicitly glossed in the text itself), `OG Ganja` / `OG weed` (several V2 crimes, e.g. `690526d8d011032eabcba233`: *"OG weed"*; `6903c1713762f71b06cc8c2d`: *"OG Ganja"*), `Booki`/`Bhukki` (Poppy Husk; crime `2032014120274`: *"the name of the powder is Booki... selling booki to the costumers"*).
- **Brand/product names embedded in a seizure description**: `"Perfect rolls"` (crime `6aad32bfae828d283f386700`: *"purchased approximately 150 grams of Ganja and two Perfect rolls"*) — a cannabis pre-roll product name, not a weight.
- **Boilerplate legal preamble before the actual narrative** is the norm, not the exception, across nearly every V2 FIR (*"IN THE COURT OF HON'BLE … ADDL. … MAGISTRATE …\nHonoured Sir,\n…"*) — any extraction or review process must look past this header, not read it as part of the seizure narrative.

### B. Quantity patterns
- **Unicode and ASCII fractions, recurring across many independent V1-era FIRs**: `"1½ Kgs"` (`64a82c057d9eb52d26107682`), `"1 1/2 Kg s"` (`2011045040022`: *"seized 1 1/2 Kg s Ganja from A1 while 2 KGs Ganja each from A2 & A3, total 5 1/2 Kgs"*), `"3 ½ Kgs"` (`2024013200523`), `"4½ KGs"` (`2023067110523`), `"6(1/2)K G"` (`2036049080062`, unit letters additionally space-split — the parenthesized-fraction pattern is fixed; the space-split unit letters are a documented remaining gap), `"2 ½ Kgs"` (`2011051120022`: *"3 and Half Kgs"*, `68a0929d5a382abc96bad4af` from the prior fix), bare `"1/2 kg"` in heavily OCR-garbled V1 text (`2023069050044`). **Fixed** in this pass.
- **"N <items> each <weight>" multiplicative phrasing** — a seized total expressed as a per-unit weight times a count, not as one combined number: *"5 Ganja packets each one 100 grams"* (`684d654af3481b499b7cd132`), *"19 packets (each packet wg about 10 grams)"* (`2032061170019`), *"10 ... boxes each consisting 11 gms"* (`2022047200568`), *"two packets of opieum each one containing 100 grams"* (`2022011210475` — "two" as an English word, not a digit, is **not** matched; see "proposed, not implemented" below). **Fixed** for the digit-count form.
- **Net/sample weight vs. total seized weight**: several FIRs separately state a small FSL *sample* weight drawn from a larger seizure (*"took three samples from each packet each 25 grams"*, `2030040210209`; *"3 plastic sachets i.e. about 10 grams each"* for FSL, distinct from the ~4½ Kg total seized, `2023067110523`). The correct quantity is the seized total, not the sample; existing extraction already appears to prefer the total in the sampled cases, no code change required, but documented as a rule for future prompt/validator review.
- **Cultivation measured by land area, not weight**: *"cultvating the Ganja plants about 1/2 acre land"* (crime `2032029020108`, raw_unit=`"acre"`). Correctly stays unsupported — "acre" has no weight/volume semantics and must never be guessed into one. No change needed; confirms the existing "only units with well-defined semantics" design is correct here.
- **Confession-stated aggregate vs. physically-verified split total**: crime `6a5a9bfc64c80e11f7912da5` — confession states *"(259) Boxes i.e. 77,700 Nitrazepam Tablets"* but the physically-weighed/counted persisted rows total 59,400 (49,200 + 10,200) across two recovery points. Not a bug — the physically-verified figures should be (and are) preferred over a suspect's self-reported aggregate; documented as a rule, not changed.

### C. Substance identification patterns
- **Ambiguous "suspected" substance, seizure/value evidence present, identity never confirmed** (confirmed recurring, 2 known crimes, `64ae1c0d19d9a10810102457` / SNo 5210: *"23 packets (46kgs) ... suspected that bundle might have Ganja"* — never confirmed by field test or FSL in the available text; `64199fba83780406e523d232` / SNo 5290: *"12 small plastic zip lock covers prohibited drug ... Tasting Salt"*, 7.19g, never named). **Fixed**: previously silently dropped with zero audit trail; now captured as `ambiguous` and classified "Manual review", never "No drug Found".
- **Confirmed only by a lab/field kit, not by name** (routine, not a gap): ABON urine-test-kit THC-positive consumption-only cases, e.g. `6a40b9708b611079d8c908e5`, `6a0c35f6adcac55da7c2b25d`. No seizure occurred, correctly "No drug Found".

### D. Non-drug property patterns (confirmed, 7+ sampled FIRs; 21 persisted rows / ~14 crimes found in a full-DB scan)
Model proposed a non-drug seized item as if it were a drug row: `Mobile Phone(s)` / `Cell Phone(s)` (`6689a48bd1e15810cabd29ed`, `66cbee219191ba469a228797`, `69aca3faed0ca88857d11371`, `6a57ff66eb9b8d146dde070c`, `67d4d3feeccbc8d10ba8a944`, `68ad4a4648d66a672348df43`, `69bb6ea454239e0c77cf9821`, `6ab9b69bb6e4ee4d0b746c6f`), `Cash` (several of the same crimes), `Vehicle` / `TVS Star City Bike` / `Car`, `Laptop`, `Polythene Cover`, `SIM Card`, a shotgun-cartridge count (`DBBL 06-cartridges`). **A second, compounding bug**: several of these then had their bare count (e.g. "2" phones, "1" vehicle) silently turned into a *weight in grams* by the no-unit fallback (`weight_g=2`, `weight_kg=0.001` for one vehicle) — the same fallback also mis-measured a genuine drug count (`LSD blots`, qty=12, became `weight_g=12`). **Both fixed**: a non-drug-property denylist rejects the former before persistence; the no-unit fallback no longer guesses a weight/volume/count for *any* item when the model supplied no unit at all.

### E. Context and association patterns
- **Multiple accused, same drug, separately weighed quantities**: crime `6965599d554831836422e0ce` — two accused, Ganja weighed separately as `1.150 Kgs` and `220 grams`; two `drug_extractions` rows are correctly created (one per accused's packet), but the second's quantity was left NULL by the model itself (it supplied a unit with no value) rather than by the validator. Per the task's own rule, a source-supported drug with a missing quantity stays "Drug found, quantity NULL" — correct as-is, no change needed. Same pattern in `2011045040022` (1½ Kg + 2 Kg + 2 Kg from three accused, confession also gives the stated total "5 1/2 Kgs").
- **Multiple distinct drugs, multiple distinct quantities, correctly kept apart**: `65c43716c97e2c932d648838` (Ganja 874.316g + MDMA 21.788g), `690526d8d011032eabcba233` (Cocaine ×3 lots + Ecstasy + MDMA + weed, six independent rows), `6903c1713762f71b06cc8c2d` (Ganja + LSD + MDMA + Cocaine). Confirms the existing per-drug extraction and association logic already handles genuinely multi-drug FIRs correctly — no change needed; included as a positive regression baseline.

## Correct interpretations / proposed extraction rules (from the above)

| Observed pattern | Naive risk | Correct rule | Deterministic? | Fixed this pass? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unicode/ASCII fraction quantities | Treated as unsupported (not a literal decimal) | Normalize ½ ¼ ¾ and `1/2` `1/4` `3/4` (bare or mixed with a leading whole number, incl. parenthesized) to a decimal before matching | Yes | **Yes** |
| "N items each X weight" | Treated as unsupported (combined total never appears literally) | Multiply count × per-item weight when "each" appears within a short, same-sentence span before the weight mention | Yes | **Yes** (digit counts only; English number words like "two" not handled) |
| Non-drug property (phone/cash/vehicle/laptop/cartridge/SIM/cover) | Persisted as a fabricated drug row | Reject by whole-word match on `raw_drug_name` before KB/persistence | Yes | **Yes** |
| No unit supplied at all | Bare count silently became a weight in grams | Never infer a unit; leave unmeasured when none was given | Yes | **Yes** |
| Ambiguous "suspected" substance with seizure/value evidence | Silently dropped, indistinguishable from "nothing happened" | Keep as `ambiguous`; crime classifies "Manual review", never "No drug Found" | Yes | **Yes** |
| Cultivation measured in land area (acres) | Could be force-converted to a bogus weight | Never assign a weight/volume for a unit with no such semantics | Yes | Already correct (no change) |
| FSL sample weight vs. total seized weight | Sample weight could overwrite the real total | Prefer the stated total/seized weight | N/A, prompt-level | Not changed (observed correct already) |
| Confession-stated aggregate vs. physically verified split | Aggregate could overwrite verified figures | Prefer the physically weighed/counted figures | N/A, prompt-level | Not changed (observed correct already) |
| OCR/phonetic spelling variants (`gunja`, `Booki`) | Below fuzzy-match threshold, KB gap | Add verified aliases | KB data, not code | **Proposed, not applied** (see below) |
| "N items each X" with an English number word ("two packets...") | Not matched (no digit) | Would need number-word parsing | Deterministic but a larger lift | **Proposed only** — single observed instance; documented, not implemented this pass |

## Cases requiring human/manual review (not force-resolved)
- The 2 known ambiguous-suspected-substance crimes (SNo 5210, 5290 / `64ae1c0d19d9a10810102457`, `64199fba83780406e523d232`) — now correctly routed to "Manual review" instead of vanishing.
- 322 of the 497 previously-unsupported crimes remain genuinely unsupported after this pass (re-verified against the fixed validator) — inspection of a sample confirms these are predominantly model-side numeric errors (a stated quantity that doesn't match anything in the text at all, or a magnitude error such as "02 grams" read as "0.02"), not text-pattern gaps. These correctly stay rejected per the task's own instruction not to force a match.
- The 21 persisted non-drug-property rows (≈14 crimes) identified by the full-DB scan are proposed for removal from `drug_extractions`, pending approval (see recovery manifest).

## New regression tests
All implemented in `etl3/tests/test_brief_facts_accuracy.py` (28 tests), tied directly to the real cases above: non-drug-property rejection (mobile phone/cash/vehicle/bike/cell phone/polythene cover/laptop/SIM card, plus a word-boundary false-positive guard for `gunja`/`Carfentanil`), the no-unit-no-guessed-weight fix, fraction parsing (4 real-shaped cases + 1 date false-positive guard), the each-multiplier pattern (+1 false-positive guard), ambiguous-substance capture (3 tests), and the crime-level Drug found/No drug Found/Manual review classification (6 tests, including precedence and backward-compatible replay of pre-fix historical attempts).
